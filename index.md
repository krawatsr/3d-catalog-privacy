# 3D Catalog — Privacy Policy

3D Catalog is a personal, non-commercial app used only by its
owner to browse
a private catalog of 3D printing models.

**Data accessed.** The app requests the Google Drive `drive.file` scope, which
gives it access only to files the app itself creates user's Google Drive
(a catalog snapshot, preview images and a log of the user's marks such as
favorites, ratings and notes). It cannot see or modify any     
other Drive files.                                   
**Use.** This data is used solely to sync the catalogthe owner's PC
and phone. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google  Drive.
                                                                **Sharing.** No data is sold, shared with or disclosed to third
parties.                                                   No analytics, advertising or tracking.

**Storage and deletion.** D
user's devices. Deleting the app's Drive folder and uninstalling the app removes
it completely. Access can be revoked at any time at
https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
